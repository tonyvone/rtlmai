Learning that costs O(new), forever

real-R7 · 6 drifting periods (70% class skew) · deletion event · measured cpu-s

The incrementality pillar of the paradigm: the state touches each raw example once, ever, stays bit-identical to full retraining at every period, and never forgets. The cheap alternative — SGD warm-start on new data — forgets old distributions (0.482 vs 0.770); the safe alternative — replay retraining — pays costs that grow with all of history (×6.0 by period six, vs ×1.6 flat). Deleting a period’s contributor: one subtraction, 269× cheaper than replay, exact to 1e-11.
